# Work in Silence: style guide

Grammar from `skills/motivation-insta-video/references/style_guide.md`, adapted for a 9:16 reframed, product-less film. Sizes are relative to frame width (1080) since 9:16 is reframed, not letterboxed.

## Palette (unchanged, no accent)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Ground | `#040404` |
| Text | `#ffffff` |
| Secondary label | `#949494` |
| Silver hero (medal) | `#b9bdc1` to `#707778`, soft white bloom |

Color only in acts 1 and 2 (drains through act 2). Act 3 on: strictly monochrome.

## Type
| Use | Family | Weight | Size (x u, u = width/1080) | Position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Counter `1,000` | Montserrat | 700 | 220 | dead center |
| Captions | Montserrat | 500 | 46 | centered, baseline at 72% of height (above Reels overlay zone) |
| End cards | Inter | 500 | 64 | dead center |
| DAY counter | JetBrains Mono | 500 | 120, label caps grey tracked +5% | center |

No caption boxes or strokes, light shadow only. Captions max 3 words, one line, hard swap.

## Structure rules kept
- Hard cuts only. Exceptions: one 15-frame dim to black (end of act 1), white flash before the name reveal, one glitch-in, one whip zoom into the medal.
- Every burst of sub-0.2s cuts paid off by a hold of 0.8s+.
- Two empty holds: the breath (2.4s caption on black) and the medal (2.8s, static).
- Stock footage keeps its own grain and motion; only a ~2% push added.
- B&W treatment: full desaturation, crushed blacks, heavy vignette on macros.

## Adaptations (no product)
- Act 6 shows *results* in B&W fragments instead of UI; the silver medal on black replaces the hero button.
- Close: two silent cards, then black. No URL, audience card or logo.

## 9:16 safe zone
Type stays out of the top 10%, bottom 20% and right 12%. Footage reframed per shot (center crop picked per clip), never a 16:9 band.
